## Brand & Style
This design system serves the community of Alto del Carmen, bridging regional public service with warm, modern, and universally accessible civic interaction. It balances institutional authority with rural hospitality and intergenerational care.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Rooted & Trustworthy:** Earthy undertones, structured forms, and stable proportions reflect dependability and municipal stewardship.
- **Generous & Dignified:** Ample hit areas, zero visual claustrophobia, and crystal-clear hierarchy welcome both digital natives and senior citizens without patronizing them.
- **Calm & Clarifying:** Surfaces breathe with natural cream tones, avoiding clinical starkness and sensory overload.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
A modern human-centered synthesis combining **Clean Scandinavian Publicism** with **Low-Contrast Organic Layering**. Interfaces prioritize functional legibility, natural olive-tinted neutral surfaces, tactile boundary lines, and assertive visual affordance.

## Colors
The palette anchors the identity in the agricultural and geographical roots of Alto del Carmen while delivering strict WCAG 2.1 AAA conformance for core operational workflows.

### Primary Brand Scale (Olive Vale)
- `primary-dark`: `#2E4521` — High-contrast text, active states, dense typography on light backgrounds.
- `primary-base`: `#3D5A2C` — Standard primary action fill, section headers, key interactive elements.
- `primary-light`: `#4B6B38` — Hover states, secondary interactive treatments.
- `primary-surface`: `#EEF4E8` — Subtle primary highlights, active navigation rows, soft badges.

### Secondary Brand Scale (Institutional Deep Blue)
- `secondary-dark`: `#162B46` — Institutional badges, formal administrative headings, table headers.
- `secondary-base`: `#1E3A5F` — Civic announcements, secondary action buttons, legal notices.
- `secondary-surface`: `#E8EEF5` — Information message containers, deep link backgrounds.

### Functional Status System (Accessible Accents)
Each status accent maintains high luminance differentiation against cream and white surfaces:
- **Success / Disponible / Asistió:** `#15803D` (Foreground text/icon), `#DCFCE7` (Surface fill).
- **Warning / Mantención / Alerta:** `#B45309` (Foreground text/icon), `#FEF3C7` (Surface fill).
- **Critical / Ausente / Agotado:** `#B91C1C` (Foreground text/icon), `#FEE2E2` (Surface fill).

### Neutrals & Background Surfaces
- `bg-base`: `#F8FAF6` — Warm cream-soft canvas for entire screens.
- `bg-elevated`: `#FFFFFF` — Cards, modals, actionable panels, and input surfaces.
- `bg-subtle`: `#F1F5E9` — Grouping containers, table zebra striping, disabled backgrounds.
- `text-main`: `#242B20` — High-legibility deep forest charcoal (13.8:1 contrast on `#F8FAF6`).
- `text-muted`: `#525D4E` — Secondary labels, helper copy (minimum 5.4:1 contrast ratio).
- `border-subtle`: `#D6DEC9` — Non-decorative separating rules, form field boundaries.
- `border-focus`: `#1E3A5F` — Outer high-visibility 3px focus ring with `#FFFFFF` inner halo.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is selected across all roles for its open counters, humanist warmth, and optical distinction between commonly confused glyphs (e.g., `1`, `l`, and `I`).

### Guidelines for Intergenerational Readability
- **Minimum Body Size:** Standard interactive reading defaults to `body-lg` (18px) or `body-md` (16px). Microcopy below 13px is strictly prohibited across the entire design system.
- **Line Heights:** Set generously between 1.5x and 1.6x the font size to prevent line-jumping for older readers and users with low vision.
- **Weights:** Use `fontWeight: 600` or `700` for all contextual labels, input titles, and key statistics to maintain solid stroke thickness on lower-resolution screens.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a responsive, content-conscious fluid grid bound by centered maximum-width containers to maintain natural eye-scanning paths.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column layout with `16px` margins and `16px` gutters. Vertical stack rule enforces single-column forms and cards.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `20px` gutters. Two-column card lists, side-by-side critical inputs.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout capped at `1280px` (`80rem`) width, `32px` margins, `24px` gutters. Administrative dashboards use an optional fixed 280px left navigation rail with fluid main content.

### Touch Target Mandate
All interactive components (buttons, dropdown triggers, checkboxes, radio clusters, breadcrumb links, and back buttons) must occupy an irreducible physical hit area of at least **48×48px** (`touch-target-min: 3rem`), regardless of visual icon or label dimensions.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system deliberately eschews heavy drop shadows and high-elevation skeuomorphism in favor of clear **tonal layering** paired with **tactile low-contrast outlines**. This ensures visual calmness and avoids confusing soft shadows with clickable elevations.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Tone `#F8FAF6`. Base surface for screen backgrounds and structural gutters.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface framed by a solid `1.5px` border in `#D6DEC9`. Adds an imperceptible ambient grounding shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(36, 43, 32, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Popovers & Tooltips):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5px` border in `#C8D3BA`, elevated with: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(36, 43, 32, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Confirmation Sheets):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` over a warm dark scrim `rgba(22, 43, 70, 0.55)` (using secondary institutional tone). Elevated with: `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(22, 43, 70, 0.18)`.

## Shapes
Roundedness Level 2 (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` on container cards) imparts a gentle, human-friendly silhouette without becoming overly playful or infantile for formal municipal records.

### Shape Applications
- **Input Fields & Buttons:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) border radius. Solid, stable visual baseline.
- **Cards, Panels & Modals:** `16px` (`1rem`) border radius. Softens content perimeter and guides the eye comfortably inward.
- **Badges & Status Chips:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) or semi-pill contours with minimum horizontal padding of `12px` to preserve text shape clarity.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Touch Target & Metrics:** Minimum height `52px` (surpassing 48px baseline). Horizontal padding `24px`.
- **Primary Button:** Background `#3D5A2C`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold `16px`. Active state `#2E4521`. Hover state `#4B6B38`.
- **Secondary (Institutional):** Background `#1E3A5F`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover `#162B46`.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Background `transparent`, border `2px solid #3D5A2C`, text `#2E4521`.
- **Focus Indication:** `3px` solid `#1E3A5F` with a `2px` offset white ring. Highly visible to keyboard navigators.

### 2. Form Inputs & Senior-Accessible Controls
- **Geometry:** Height `56px`, horizontal padding `16px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #D6DEC9`.
- **Typography:** Value text set at `18px` (`body-lg`) to maximize input verification for seniors. Placeholder text in `#525D4E` with explicit example prompts.
- **Labels:** Always placed strictly above the input (never floating, never replaced by placeholders). Label size `16px` bold with high contrast `#242B20`.
- **Helper & Validation Text:** Minimum `14px` bold, accompanied by an inline icon (`18px`) for immediate status clarity (e.g., checkmark for valid, exclamation triangle for error).

### 3. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Touch Area:** Dedicated `52px` container height with the control embedded alongside full-width text clickability.
- **Physical Size:** Checkbox/Radio size `26×26px` (larger than traditional 16px) with a heavy `2.5px` border `#3D5A2C`. Checkmark stroke width `3px` in pure white.

### 4. Status Badges & Chips
- **Design Rule:** Never rely on color alone. Pair every state with a descriptive text label and a standard SVG icon.
- **Disponible / Asistió:** `#DCFCE7` background, `#15803D` text & icon, `1px solid #86EFAC`.
- **Mantención / Alerta:** `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text & icon, `1px solid #FCD34D`.
- **Ausente / Agotado:** `#FEE2E2` background, `#B91C1C` text & icon, `1px solid #FCA5A5`.

### 5. Cards & Community Panels
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #D6DEC9`, padding `24px` (mobile) to `32px` (desktop).
- **Header Section:** Incorporates clear visual dividers (`1px solid #F1F5E9`) separating administrative headers from body descriptions and direct CTA triggers.

### 6. Senior & Assisted Mode Bar (Community Specialized)
- An always-accessible utility banner fixed at the top-right header offering immediate one-click toggles: **"Texto Grande (+)"** (scales typography tokens up by 20%) and **"Alto Contraste"** (shifts subtle borders to heavy `#242B20` borders).
